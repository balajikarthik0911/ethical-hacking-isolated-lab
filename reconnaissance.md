# Local Reconnaissance

## Scope

All reconnaissance activities were performed only within the authorized and isolated cybersecurity lab environment.

## Objective

The objective is to identify the authorized lab host, network information, and available services within the isolated environment.

## Reconnaissance Activities

- Verified the authorized lab environment.
- Identified the lab host.
- Checked network connectivity.
- Identified services available on the authorized lab target.
- Recorded observations for the attack-surface map.

## Findings

| Item | Observation |
|---|---|
| Target | Authorized lab target |
| Network | Isolated lab network |
| Services | Services identified during lab testing |
| Exposure | Internal lab environment only |

## Safety

No unauthorized systems, public networks, or third-party targets were tested.
